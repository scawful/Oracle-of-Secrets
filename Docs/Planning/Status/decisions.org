* DECIDED Villain chain: prison, Farore, Kydrog, Twinrova, Ganon
  :PROPERTIES:
  :ASKED_BY: Claude story/design discussion (rule-provenance audit)
  :DOC:      [[file:../story_canon_beat_sheet.md]]
  :END:
  Replaces beat-sheet crux 2's "Ganon residue"/garrison reading, the crux 4
  wordless-beast coda, and AGENTS.md rule 4 ("no Ganondorf-origin or
  two-villain plotlines"), which was agent-written with no scawful source.
  1. Farore warns the island's scholars, then turns to Hyrule; Hyrule sends a garrison "to
     guard the Golden Power" (islanders see an invasion; msg 0x112 stands).
  2. Farore makes Kydrog, a garrison knight, the prison's guardian and binds
     her power into his sword as the seal's key. She knew him personally.
  3. The prison corrupts him; he dies, rises as the Stalfos Pirate King; his
     dead men are his crew. He abandons the sword (the Meadow Blade).
  4. The prison holds the half-revived beast Ganon from the Oracle games'
     failed revival (msg 0x136). Twinrova hunt essences to finish it and die
     in D5; Kydrog carries the revival on and kidnaps Farore's body to force
     the key.

     his men back. His pirate-skeleton humor is a coping mechanism for his
     death and his men's deaths.
  6. Finale: Kydreeok = the prison's corruption of him, not his heart. Song of
     Healing frees the knight (post-healing form: human). Then Ganon breaks
     out and alternates between Ganondorf (energy-ball volleys) and the beast,
     per msg 0x15B "reshapes you to your heart". Vanilla Ganon parts may be
     reused.

  bad stuff"; "given this guardian angle perhaps she should know more about

  being a coping mechanism for his death and his mens death"; final boss: "a
  ganonDORF fight with the demons volley could be cool too. perhaps switch
  between dorf and pig".
